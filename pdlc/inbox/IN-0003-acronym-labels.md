# IN-0003 · Labels keep acronyms together

Status: new · Ticket: none · Branch: none · Added: 2026-10-19

## What and why

`labelOf` splits at every capital letter, so `URL` becomes "U r l". Keep a run of capitals together, so `URL` gives "URL" and `HasURL` gives "Has URL".
